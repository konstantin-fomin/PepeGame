TEXT_CONTEXT (CANONICAL)

PROJECT
- 2D idle / clicker
- Office / IT theme
- Target audience: IT specialists, office workers (20–40)

TONE & VOICE
- Dry office sarcasm
- Subtle irony
- Burnout, routine, quiet despair
- Absurdity treated as normal
- Humor is restrained, not loud
- No jokes-for-jokes-sake

STYLE RULES
- Short phrases
- Simple sentence structure
- Neutral or passive tone
- Often sounds like internal corporate text
- Slight emotional detachment
- Text should feel “tired, but functional”

LANGUAGE
- Russian
- Conversational but not slang-heavy
- IT / office vocabulary allowed
- No memes
- No emojis
- No profanity

CARD TEXTS
- Title: short, punchy, neutral or ironic
- Description: 1 short sentence
- Often sounds like a warning, note, or internal comment
- Avoid exclamation marks
- Avoid rhetorical questions

EXAMPLES OF GOOD TONE
- «Работает. Не спрашивай как.»
- «Лучше не трогать.»
- «Временно. Уже навсегда.»
- «Так принято.»

FORBIDDEN
- Motivational tone
- Overly emotional text
- Loud humor
- Explanations of jokes
- Meta-comments about gameplay
